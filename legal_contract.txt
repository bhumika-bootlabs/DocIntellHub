LEGAL SERVICE AGREEMENT

This agreement is made between the Client and the Legal Service Provider.

The Legal Service Provider agrees to provide consultation and legal advice
regarding corporate compliance and contract drafting.

The Client agrees to provide all necessary documentation required for the
legal review process.

All confidential information shared between the Client and the Lawyer must
remain protected under attorney-client privilege.

Payment for legal services must be completed within 30 days of receiving
the invoice.

Any disputes arising from this agreement shall be resolved under the
jurisdiction of the applicable civil court.

Payment Terms:
The client shall make full payment of all invoices within thirty (30) days from the date of receipt. Failure to comply may result in penalties or service suspension.

Termination Clause:
Either party may terminate this agreement by providing a written notice of thirty (30) days. All outstanding obligations must be settled prior to termination.
