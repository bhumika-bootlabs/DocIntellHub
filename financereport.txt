\f0\fs24 \cf0 Loan Policy Document \'96 Retail Banking Division\
\
This document outlines the policies governing personal and business loans issued by the bank. All loan approvals are subject to credit evaluation, risk assessment, and compliance with regulatory guidelines.\
\
1. Loan Eligibility:\
Applicants must have a minimum credit score of 700. Salaried individuals must provide proof of employment and income for the past 6 months. Self-employed individuals must submit audited financial statements for the past 2 years.\
\
2. Loan Types:\
The bank offers personal loans, home loans, and business loans. Interest rates vary depending on the loan category and applicant risk profile.\
\
3. Interest Rates:\
Interest rates range from 8% to 14% annually. Fixed and floating rate options are available. Floating rates are linked to the central bank benchmark rate and are subject to periodic revision.\
\
4. Loan Tenure:\
Loan tenure ranges from 1 year to 25 years depending on the type of loan. Home loans typically have longer tenures, while personal loans are shorter.\
\
5. Repayment Terms:\
Repayments must be made through monthly EMIs (Equated Monthly Installments). Late payments will incur a penalty of 2% per month on the overdue amount.\
\
6. Prepayment and Foreclosure:\
Borrowers are allowed to prepay loans partially or fully. A foreclosure charge of up to 3% may apply depending on the loan agreement.\
\
7. Risk Assessment:\
The bank uses a risk scoring model based on income stability, credit history, debt-to-income ratio, and employment type.\
\
8. Default and Recovery:\
If a borrower fails to repay for 90 consecutive days, the loan will be classified as a Non-Performing Asset (NPA). Legal recovery procedures may be initiated.\
\
9. Documentation:\
Mandatory documents include identity proof, address proof, income proof, and bank statements.\
\
10. Compliance:\
All loans must comply with regulatory requirements and internal audit policies.}
